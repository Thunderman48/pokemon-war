CHOOSE DIFFICULTY

ONLINE 1V1

Host a game and send the 5-letter code to a friend. Both players need an internet connection. The host's device runs the match, so keep the game open in front.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: destroy every enemy Pokémon Center. When a player's last Pokémon Center falls, they lose. Build a second one to stay safe!

Squads: battle Pokémon are trained in squads (4 small Pokémon, 3 Geodude, 2 Snorlax, 1 Rayquaza) that move and fight together and share XP, so the whole squad evolves at once. When a member falls, the squad brings in a replacement once it has been out of combat for a few seconds, for a small price. A squad uses supply for all its members.

Economy: Machop workers gather berries and crystals. Poké Marts add supply. The Gym trains battle Pokémon, the Lab does all research (and unlocks Pikachu and Snorlax at the Gym), and the Sky Pillar trains flying Pokémon and the legendary Rayquaza.

Evolution: Pokémon gain XP by dealing damage. To evolve they need the XP and the research: e.g. research Charmeleon at the Lab, then Charizard. Stones unlock Raichu and Eevee's forms. When an Eevee is ready, select it and pick Vaporeon, Jolteon or Flareon.

Mega Evolution: research the Key Stone at the Lab (needs a Sky Pillar). A fully evolved Charizard, Blastoise, Venusaur or Pidgeot squad whose held item is its Mega Stone (Charizardite and so on) can press MEGA: the squad leader transforms (invincible for 2 seconds) and stays Mega for 60 seconds, then the squad waits before it can do it again. Rayquaza needs no stone. Every Mega has a special effect: Mega Rayquaza's storm darkens the sky, blasts enemies back and strikes them with lightning; Mega Charizard Y brings harsh sunlight that burns enemies and powers up Fire attacks; Mega Blastoise fires water cannons whose splash heals allies; Mega Venusaur roots enemies and spreads a healing pollen field; Mega Pidgeot's tornado pulls enemies in and its attacks never miss.

Held items: each Pokémon in your deck holds one item, shared by its squads: Mega Stones, Leftovers, Choice Band, Quick Claw, Rocky Helmet, Eviolite, Expert Belt, Focus Sash, Scope Lens, Shell Bell and Amulet Coin.

Research: everything is researched at the Lab. Tap the Lab to see its pages: GENERAL (Attack/Defense levels, Harvest, Big Bag, Mart Expansion, Fortify, Training Drill, Tower Upgrade) and one page per Pokémon with its evolutions and skills.

Healing: Pokémon that stay out of combat for 5 seconds regenerate 2% of their max HP per second. Pokémon Centers heal nearby Pokémon faster.

Skills: every Pokémon has 3 skills, and each one is researched at the Lab. Passive skills (dark ring) are always on, like Blaze or Delta Stream. Active skills (white ring) like Flamethrower, Thunder or Dragon Ascent are used automatically when a good target is near. Select a squad and tap AUTO to turn that off, then tap a skill and the target area to use it yourself. A squad shares one cooldown per skill. Status effects: burn and poison hurt over time, paralysis and sleep stop a Pokémon, knock-back pushes them away.

Flying: Pidgey, Pidgeot, Charizard and Rayquaza fly over trees and water. Close-range fighters (Machop, Snorlax) can't hit them, so use ranged Pokémon, Geodude or Towers.

Types: Fire > Grass > Water > Fire. Electric > Water & Flying. Rock > Fire & Flying. Flying > Grass & Fighting. Fighting > Normal & Rock. Dragon > Dragon. Super-effective hits deal 1.5× damage.

Touch controls:

  • Tap a Pokémon or building to select it. Double-tap to select all of that kind on screen.
  • With Pokémon selected, tap the ground to move (they fight what they meet), tap an enemy to attack, or tap a berry bush/crystal to gather.
  • Drag to scroll. Pinch to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
  • Press and hold, then drag, to box-select (or use the BOX button).
  • ARMY selects all fighters. IDLE selects an idle worker. BASE jumps home.
  • With a building selected, tap the ground to set its rally point. MORE flips through extra buttons.

Mouse: left-click selects, drag box-selects, right-click commands, wheel zooms, WASD/arrows scroll.
