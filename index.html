POKéMONWAR

Free, non-profit fan game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc. or The Pokémon Company. Pokémon and character names are trademarks of their respective owners.

CHOOSE DIFFICULTY

ONLINE 1V1

Host a game and send the 5-letter code to a friend. Both players need an internet connection. The host's device runs the match, so keep the game open in front.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: destroy every enemy building.

Economy: Machop workers gather berries and crystals. Build a Poké Mart for more supply, a Gym to train battle Pokémon, and a Lab for Pikachu, Snorlax, upgrades and final evolutions.

Types: Fire > Grass > Water > Fire. Electric > Water. Fighting > Normal. Super-effective hits deal 1.5× damage.

Evolution: Pokémon gain XP by dealing damage and evolve, getting stronger. Final evolutions need a Lab.

Touch controls:

  • Tap a Pokémon or building to select it. Double-tap to select all of that kind on screen.
  • With Pokémon selected, tap the ground to move (they fight what they meet), tap an enemy to attack, or tap a berry bush/crystal to gather.
  • Drag to scroll. Pinch to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
  • Press and hold, then drag, to box-select (or use the BOX button).
  • ARMY selects all fighters. IDLE selects an idle worker. BASE jumps home.
  • With a building selected, tap the ground to set its rally point.

Mouse: left-click selects, drag box-selects, right-click commands, wheel zooms, WASD/arrows scroll.
